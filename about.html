Dallas Party Bike

Fun on wheels with great people

Six party bikes, 15 seats each, and a crew of captains who bring the party to Deep Ellum.

Born in Deep Ellum

We roll out of 3909 Elm St, right in the heart of Deep Ellum — famous for its live music, street art and nightlife. Our bikes are pedal-powered with an electric assist, fully loaded with a Bluetooth sound system and LED party lights.

Groups from 6 to 90 ride with us for birthdays, bachelorettes, bachelor parties, family reunions and corporate team building.
